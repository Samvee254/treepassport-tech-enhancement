# Problem Definition

## Background
I met a developer associated with the TreePassport platform at a
technology/innovation workshop. After discussing my studies (Cloud
Computing and Information Security) and interests (Python, APIs,
databases, AI/ML, cybersecurity, dashboards, GIS, data analytics),
he directed me to explore TreePassport and consider how technology
could contribute to it, particularly around tagging and reporting.

## What this project is
An independent technology enhancement prototype inspired by the
problems TreePassport solves. It is NOT an attempt to rebuild,
clone, or claim ownership of TreePassport's existing technology.

## Goal
Demonstrate, through a working proof-of-concept, that I investigated
the platform, understood its workflow, and identified practical
technological improvements I could contribute - specifically:

    1. Tree Health Risk Engine - explainable risk scoring from
       monitoring history, flagging trees before visible decline.
    2. Audit Trail - tamper-evident logging of record changes,
       with automatic flagging of sensitive-field edits.

## Success criteria
- Working MVP demonstrating both signature features end-to-end
- Clear documentation distinguishing "existing TreePassport
  functionality" from "my proposed enhancement" throughout
- A presentation structured as: What I Found -> What I Added -> Why It Matters
